25. DATA PRIVACY AND COMPLIANCE
===============================

Because the platform processes employee personal data, location data, attendance data, and potentially biometric placeholders:

Do not implement real face recognition unless explicit consent flow exists.
Add consent placeholders:
Employee consent to location tracking during clock in/out
Employee consent to kiosk photo/face verification if enabled in future
Privacy policy placeholder
Data retention settings
Data export placeholder
Data deletion/deactivation workflow

Location tracking rule:
Only capture location at clock in/out.
Do not track employee continuously.
Do not show live GPS tracking unless explicitly built and consented.

Biometric rule:
Face verification must remain placeholder unless compliance steps are implemented.
Do not store biometric templates in MVP.

Add compliance notes in README:
Not legal advice.
Production use requires legal review for Egypt/MENA data protection.
Employer must inform employees and obtain required consents.
